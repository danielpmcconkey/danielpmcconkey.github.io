Privacy policy — rclone-backup

rclone-backup is used by one person, its owner, on his own Google account.

  • It only reads and writes files it created itself in the owner's Google Drive (the drive.file scope).
  • It collects no data from anyone, shares nothing with anyone, and has no other users.
  • The files it stores are encrypted before upload; Google holds only ciphertext.
